```text

```

**Why:** The 40-word failing sentence is split into three full stops, and the abstract 'pursue the knowledge of the LORD' becomes 'keep seeking the LORD'. 'as certain as the morning dawn' is now 'as surely as the sunrise', and 'life-restoring rain upon penitent hearts' becomes 'new life to the person who turns back to Him'. Hosea's rain picture (latter and former rain) is carried in the words 'the rain that waters the dry ground'. The label 'First principle:' is kept verbatim and no claim is added or dropped.
