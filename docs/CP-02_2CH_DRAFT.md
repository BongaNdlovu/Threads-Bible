### `2ch-7-16` · `principle`

**BEFORE**

```text
Name, eyes, and heart perpetually there. First principle: the temple is chosen, sanctified, and watched — eyes and heart of God resident; a promise Jesus honors by cleansing what was profaned.
```

**AFTER**

```text
"My name may be there for ever": God's name, his eyes, and his heart stay in that house. First principle: God chose the temple and set it apart. He promised to watch over it always. The Gospels then tell how Jesus honored that promise by clearing out what men had made unclean.
```

**Why:** the label "Name, eyes, and heart perpetually there" now says who is there and where, and the em-dash pile "the temple is chosen, sanctified, and watched — eyes and heart of God resident" gives way to sentences with God as the subject. The KJV words are quoted rather than paraphrased (2 Chr 7:16, "my name may be there for ever"). Claims kept: the name, eyes, and heart remain there, the temple is chosen and sanctified, it is watched, and Jesus honored the promise by cleansing what was profaned.
