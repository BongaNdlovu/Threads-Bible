
### `2jn-1-7` · `principle`

**BEFORE**

```text

```

**AFTER**


**Why:** 'The flesh-denial marks the deceiver and the antichrist' becomes a sentence naming who does what, and 'the incarnation is the test of orthodoxy' becomes 'the test of right belief'. The long dash clause is split so that 'already in the world' stands in its own sentence. The words 'is come in the flesh' are carried byte-identical to the source and to the KJV clause 'that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh' in canon 2 John 1:7; the lower case 'is' is the source's own rendering, not mine, and the phrase is left unquoted exactly as the source had it.
