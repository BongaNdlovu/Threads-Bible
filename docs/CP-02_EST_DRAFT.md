## 1 · Rewritten strings (BEFORE → AFTER)

### `est-8-17` · `principle`

**BEFORE**

```text
Joy, gladness, feast, and conversions across the empire. First principle: reversal day — the decree of doom became a day of fear-falling conversions; the Jewish feast drew the nations in.
```

**AFTER**

```text
The Jews had joy and gladness, a feast and a good day across the empire. First principle: the day of doom turned into a day of reversal. Many people became Jews because fear of the Jews fell on them, and the Jewish feast drew the nations in.
```

**Why:** "reversal day — the decree of doom became a day of fear-falling conversions" was one compressed image, so it is now two plain statements, and the four-word opening list becomes a sentence with the Jews as its subject (Esth 8:17). "conversions" is replaced by what the verse actually records, that many people became Jews, "fear-falling" is written out as fear of the Jews falling on them, and "joy and gladness, a feast and a good day" is carried byte-identical as an unquoted KJV clause with the scope word "across the empire" added. God is not named here, because the source does not name him in this string and Esther never does.

### `est-4-14` · `principle`

**BEFORE**

```text
Silence would destroy; deliverance would arise from another place. First principle: providence positions — Mordecai's question makes fasting courage: perhaps the throne room is why you exist.
```

**AFTER**

```text
If Esther kept silent, she and her father's house would be destroyed. But rescue would rise for the Jews from another place. First principle: God's care had put her in that place. Mordecai's question turns her fasting into courage. Perhaps the throne room is the very reason you exist.
```

**Why:** "Silence would destroy; deliverance would arise from another place" gave no subject to either verb, so Esther and her father's house are named first, then the rescue; "providence positions" becomes "God's care had put her in that place" with a subject and a verb, and the em-dash pile "Mordecai's question makes fasting courage: perhaps the throne room is why you exist" is split so each sentence carries one idea. "Thou art come" becomes second-person plain words ("you exist"), which is the voice rule for personal relevance. Claims kept: silence destroys, the father's house is destroyed with her, deliverance arises from another place, providence places a person, Mordecai's question turns fasting into courage, and the throne room may be the reason she exists.
