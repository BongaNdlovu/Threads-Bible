The early rain came at Pentecost and started the Christian church growing. The latter rain comes at the end of time to ripen the harvest of the earth for Christ's return. First principle: a spiritual harvest depends entirely on God pouring out his grace at the times he has set.

**Why:** V1 fixed: the 43-word sentence that failed the gate is split, and the longest sentence is now 20 words. V2: the colon chain is broken into one idea per sentence in plain word order. V3: salvation history, agricultural rhythm models and divine grace are replaced with everyday words. The transliterations yoreh and malqosh and the label First principle: are kept verbatim (I1).
