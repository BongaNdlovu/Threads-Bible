### `jud-1-5` · `principle`

**BEFORE**

```text
The saved-out generation died in the wilderness for unbelief. First principle: past salvation does not immunize against future judgment — the exodus ended at Kadesh for the faithless.
```

**AFTER**

```text
The people the Lord brought out of Egypt died in the wilderness because they did not believe. First principle: being saved in the past is no protection from judgment later. The exodus journey ended at Kadesh for the faithless.
```

**Why:** The compound 'saved-out generation' becomes a clause naming the Lord as the actor who brought the people out, and the object 'Egypt' is the land the source's own title names. 'Died in the wilderness for unbelief' becomes 'died in the wilderness because they did not believe', which replaces the noun 'unbelief' with the everyday verb. The abstraction 'immunize' is dropped for 'is no protection', and the dash clause is split so the warning and its example each stand on their own. 'Kadesh' and 'faithless' are kept.
