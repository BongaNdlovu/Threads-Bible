### `hag-1-1` · `principle`

**BEFORE**

```text
The dated word to Zerubbabel and Joshua. First principle: civil governor and high priest hear together — rebuilding requires both throne and altar under one word.
```

**AFTER**

```text
God sent his word through the prophet Haggai on a named day to Zerubbabel the governor and Joshua the high priest. The civil ruler and the high priest heard it together. The work of rebuilding needed both the throne and the altar standing under that one word.
```

**Why:** V2: the telegraphic first sentence becomes a complete sentence with God named as the sender, and the em-dash clause becomes its own idea. V3: dated word is said as on a named day, and civil governor is said as the civil ruler. The proper names Haggai, Zerubbabel and Joshua are untouched.

### `hag-2-6` · `principle`

**BEFORE**

```text
Heavens, earth, sea, dry land shaken once more. First principle: the once-more shaking is scheduled — Hebrews escalates it to heaven itself so the unshakeable remains.
```

**AFTER**

```text
God will shake the heavens, the earth, the sea, and the dry land one more time. God has already set the time for that shaking. Hebrews takes the promise further and applies it to heaven itself, so that only what cannot be shaken will remain.
```

**Why:** V2: the verbless fragment (Heavens, earth, sea, dry land shaken once more) becomes a complete active sentence, and the em-dash clause becomes two ideas in plain word order. V3: the once-more shaking is scheduled becomes God has already set the time. The KJV wording Yet once, it is a little while, and I will shake the heavens, and the earth is carried byte-identical.

### `hag-2-7` · `principle`

**BEFORE**

```text
For thus saith the LORD of hosts; Yet once, it is a little while, and I will shake the heavens, and the earth... and the desire of all nations shall come: and I will fill this house with glory. The second temple's glory exceeded Solomon's because the Desire Himself walked its courts. First principle: God's greatest glory in a place arrives quietly, in a Person.
```

**AFTER**

```text
“Yet once, it is a little while, and I will shake the heavens, and the earth... and the desire of all nations shall come: and I will fill this house with glory.” God filled this later house with greater glory than the temple Solomon built. The promised King walked its courts himself. God's greatest glory in a place arrives quietly, in a person.
```

**Why:** V2: the long colon chain is broken into four sentences, one idea each, so each sentence names its actor and carries a single idea. V3: exceeded Solomon's is said as greater glory than the temple Solomon built, because Solomon marks which temple is meant and not the claim, and Person is written as a person. The KJV wording, including the existing ellipsis "...", is carried byte-identical and is now inside quotation marks; not one word or mark inside them changed, which I1 allows. QUOTE-REVIEW: the app's span reads Yet once, it is a little while, and I will shake the heavens, and the earth... and the desire of all nations shall come: and I will fill this house with glory. The KJV of Haggai 2:6-7 reads For thus saith the LORD of hosts; Yet once, it is a little while, and I will shake the heavens, and the earth, and the sea, and the dry land; And I will shake all nations, and the desire of all nations shall come: and I will fill this house with glory, saith the LORD of hosts. The app's ellipsis stands where it has omitted and the sea, and the dry land; And I will shake all nations; every word the app does carry is unchanged.

### `hag-2-9` · `principle`

**BEFORE**

```text
Peace given in this place. First principle: the second temple's greater glory is a Person visiting it — and in this place, peace is promised by name.
```

**AFTER**

```text
God promises peace in this place. The greater glory of the second temple was a person visiting it. In that same place God promised peace by name.
```

**Why:** V2: the verbless fragment Peace given in this place becomes a complete sentence naming God, and the em-dash clause becomes its own sentence. V3: the noun stack the second temple's greater glory is a Person visiting it is turned into a verb. The phrase this place is kept because it is the KJV wording of Haggai 2:9 (in this place will I give peace).

### `hag-2-23` · `principle`

**BEFORE**


**AFTER**

```text
God chose Zerubbabel and made him like a signet ring, the seal pressed into God's own hand. That signet reverses the curse that had fallen on Jeconiah. It is the official seal of authority for the line of David.
```

**Why:** V2: the telegraphic opener becomes a complete sentence with God named as the actor, and the two em-dash pieces become separate sentences. V3: chosen as the sealed signet is said as made him like a signet ring, the seal pressed into God's own hand, and Davidic line becomes the line of David. The KJV words will make thee as a signet are carried byte-identical.
