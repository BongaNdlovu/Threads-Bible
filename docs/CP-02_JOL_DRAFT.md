'in the last days' is carried word for word.

### `jol-2-32` · `principle`


**Why:** 'shall be delivered/saved' forced the reader to choose between two words, and the slash is gone: the sentence now says 'will be saved'. 'there shall be deliverance' became 'deliverance will be in Mount Zion and in Jerusalem'. The abstract 'salvation is universal to all who invoke the Name in faith' becomes 'God saves everyone who calls on Him in faith', which names God as the actor. Both apostolic citations and the claim they carry are kept.

The coined words 'reverse-industry' and 'prophet-era' are gone, and the contrast with Isaiah's peace vision is stated plainly instead of in shorthand. The label 'First principle:' is verbatim.
